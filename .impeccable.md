## Design Context

### Users

Developers meeting Decide through a link, workshop, or teammate. Show a typed
answer with a probability, then help them get a first result in minutes.
Returning users need a tutorial for their job and a searchable reference.

### Brand Personality

Clear, calm, deliberate. Teach with real commands and results. These principles
come from the approved documentation-site PRD and design in `docs/`.

### Aesthetic Direction

Follow `docs/design/docs-site-look.md`: neutral grays, Inter for prose,
JetBrains Mono for commands and answers, light and dark themes. Only terminals
and code stay dark in light mode. Cyan and verdict colors mean answers.

### Design Principles

1. Show what Decide prints. Recordings and transcripts come from real runs.
2. Keep the headline, terminal, and install commands on the first desktop screen.
3. Teach one job per tutorial, ending in something the reader can use.
4. Play one recording at a time. Reduced motion shows still images until Play.
5. Use neutral controls, short copy, and legible text on phones.
